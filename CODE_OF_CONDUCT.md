# Code of Conduct

The CABOC project adopts the **Contributor Covenant 2.1** as its code of
conduct.

- Full text: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
- French translation: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/fr/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces — repository (issues, PRs,
discussions, code), community channels, events, and one-on-one
interactions when representing the project.

## Our pledge in short

We commit to making participation in the CABOC project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Reporting

Report violations to **conduct@caboc.example**. Reports are confidential.
Maintainers will respond within five business days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the **Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines**
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines>):
correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban, scaled to severity
and pattern.
